One night with Uki

You connect your sources and put the phone on charge. The rest happens by itself.

  1. daytime You connect your sources A Telegram export, notes, documents, voice messages. Once.
  2. 23:40 Phone goes on charge Uki gets to work. Nothing more is needed from you.
  3. 23:45 Clean-up Passwords, card numbers and other people’s contacts are removed automatically.
  4. 00:30 Building examples Your texts become question-and-answer pairs and samples of your style.
  5. 01:00 Training A small open model is fine-tuned right on the phone.
  6. 07:00 Check The before / after screen shows you what changed.

* Times are illustrative and depend on the phone and the amount of data.

Questions

Which phones will it run on?

iPhone first. Android comes next.

Will my phone overheat?

Training only runs while the phone is charging and you are asleep. Heat and battery use are the first things we test.

Will the model get worse at everything else?

After every night the model takes a set of general tests. If something drops, the training is rolled back.

Do I need internet?

No. There is an offline mode, and your data never leaves the phone either way.
